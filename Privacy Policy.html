Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026 · Applies to Tailapt for iOS

Tailapt is an AI-powered teleprompter and video creation app for iPhone. This policy explains what information we process and how we handle it.

Short version

  • Scripts and most settings stay on your device.
  • Camera and microphone are used only when you record or enable voice tracking.
  • AI script generation and some speech features may send data to secure services.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

1. Who we are

Tailapt is developed and published by Muhammad Tahir (“we”, “us”, “our”). Contact: tb.companyapps@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

2.1 Information you provide

  • Scripts and text you write or generate
  • Titles, notes, and teleprompter settings (speed, font size, mirror, etc.)
  • Optional feedback or support messages you send us by email

2.2 Camera and microphone

When you use the recording studio or voice tracking:

  • Camera — used to record video. Video is processed on your device and stored in the app’s container (or shared only if you choose to export/share).
  • Microphone — used for voice tracking (scrolling the teleprompter as you speak) and for generating captions from your recording.

Access is requested only when needed. You can revoke permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Tailapt.

2.3 Speech recognition and AI

  • Voice tracking may use Apple’s speech recognition (on-device when available, or Apple’s speech service). Audio is processed to match your spoken words to the script so the teleprompter can follow you.
  • Caption generation converts recorded speech into timed text. This may use on-device processing or a speech service.
  • AI script writing — when you request AI help to write or improve a script, the text you provide is sent to a secure backend so the AI can generate a response. We do not use this content for advertising or to train models for other users.

We disclose these uses before first use of the relevant features where required.

2.4 Device and technical information

  • Device type, iOS version, and app version (used for support and diagnostics)
  • Local preferences (theme, language, default camera, recording quality, etc.)

2.5 Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple. We receive only non-identifying confirmation that a purchase is active so we can unlock Pro features. We never see your payment details.

3. How we use information

We use the information solely to:

  • Provide the teleprompter, recording, editing, captioning, and AI writing features
  • Remember your scripts and settings
  • Unlock and manage Premium features
  • Respond to support requests
  • Improve the app and fix bugs

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data for advertising or marketing.

4. Data storage

  • Scripts, settings, and practice history are stored locally on your device.
  • Recordings are kept in the app’s Documents folder (or temporary storage in this prototype).
  • If you enable cloud sync (Premium feature), scripts may be synced via Apple’s CloudKit under your Apple ID.

You can delete scripts and clear data from within the app. Deleting the app removes local data.

5. Data sharing

We share data only in these limited cases:

  • With Apple for purchases, speech recognition (when on-device is not used), and CloudKit (if you enable sync)
  • With the AI backend when you request script generation (only the text needed for that request)
  • When required by law
  • With your explicit action (for example, when you export or share a video via the system share sheet)

6. Third-party services

  • Apple — App Store, In-App Purchase, speech recognition, CloudKit, and system services. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • AI processing — text you submit for script generation is processed by our secure backend / AI provider solely to fulfill your request.

We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs that track you across apps.

7. Children’s privacy

Tailapt is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Edit or delete scripts inside the app
  • Revoke camera and microphone access in iOS Settings
  • Turn off notifications and cloud sync
  • Cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings
  • Request deletion of any data we hold by emailing us

9. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect data. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure. Keep your device updated and protected with a passcode or biometric lock.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The latest version will be posted with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
