About Tailapt

AI Teleprompter for iOS

Tailapt helps you write scripts, read them on a smooth teleprompter, record with your camera, and create captioned videos — all in one place.

Our mission

We built Tailapt so creators, educators, and professionals can look confident on camera without memorizing every line. The focus is a clean, private, and practical experience that respects your time and your data.

What Tailapt offers

  • Script editor — Write, organize, and refine your scripts
  • AI writing assistance — Generate or improve scripts with AI (Premium)
  • Teleprompter — Smooth scrolling, adjustable speed, font size, mirror mode, and more
  • Voice tracking — The script follows your spoken words (Premium)
  • Camera recording — Front or rear camera with quality controls
  • Video tools — Trim, crop, speed, filters, titles, and automatic captions
  • Export & share — Save to Photos or share your finished video

Important notes

  • Camera and microphone access are required only when you record or use voice tracking.
  • AI-generated text is a starting point — always review and edit it before recording.
  • Speech recognition accuracy depends on your environment, accent, and device capabilities.
  • Tailapt is not affiliated with Apple, TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, or any other platform.
