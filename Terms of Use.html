2. What the app does

Tailapt is an AI teleprompter and video creation tool for iPhone. It lets you:

  • Write and organize scripts (with optional AI assistance)
  • Read scripts on a teleprompter while recording with the front or rear camera
  • Use voice tracking so the script scrolls as you speak
  • Generate captions, trim, crop, adjust speed, add titles and filters, then export video
